The agents · planned

A bench of specialists, each with one job.

Each agent works only within what the signed-in lawyer may see, and hands its work to a person. None is built yet.

PlannedIN

Intake

Reads incoming email and documents and files them to the right matter.

"Payment reply from the client's finance team, filed to 2214/2026 with 3 attachments."
PlannedDR

Drafting

Prepares first drafts: statements of claim, memoranda, letters.

"Statement of claim, draft 1: Facts, Requests, Exhibits."
PlannedFG

Figures

Brings amounts together. Every figure is calculated by code, never by the model.

"4 invoices · AED 1,250,000 · matches the ledger."
PlannedDL

Deadlines

Counts dates only from verified, jurisdiction-specific rules, open to correction.

"Filing window found. Marked 'needs verification' until a lawyer confirms."
PlannedCO

Court operations

Prepares registration and filing tasks for your court officers.

"Registration task ready: brief, fee, two exhibits."
PlannedEX

Execution

Follows execution files: applications, orders, amounts collected.

"Handover returned: certified copy of the judgment missing."
AlwaysYOU

The lawyer decides

Approves, corrects or returns. Nothing reaches a client or a court without a named person.

"Approved for filing · L. Haddad, Partner."

Agents draft and propose. People approve and send.
